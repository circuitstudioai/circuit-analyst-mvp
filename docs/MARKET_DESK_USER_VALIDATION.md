# Market Desk user validation

**Status:** Protocol ready. No moderated sessions have been run, and no results are recorded here.

This is the Milestone 4 script for the five-company preview. Do not fill the scorecard with assumed answers. A session counts only when a target-profile participant completes it with a moderator.

## Who to recruit

Self-directed US public-equity investors who follow about 5–25 companies, or a serious prospective investor who has not built a portfolio yet. Run at least five sessions.

## Session script

1. Open the five-company preview with no preamble about the internal names.
2. Ask the participant to say, without help, what the first inbox item changed and which claim it affects.
3. Ask them to point to the system thesis, a model decision in Decision Lab, and their own judgment. These must stay distinct.
4. Ask them to open supporting evidence and say whether it is current, stale, partial, or missing.
5. If the chart is visible, ask them to select one marker and explain the linked event. If the price provider failed, record that the chart was unavailable and do not score the marker task as a pass.
6. Time how long it takes to say why the top inbox item matters.
7. Invite, but do not prompt, a judgment or watch condition.
8. Ask whether they would return to the Change Inbox, a blank chat, or a static watchlist for ongoing monitoring.
